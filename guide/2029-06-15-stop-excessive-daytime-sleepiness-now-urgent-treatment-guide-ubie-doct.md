Explanation

Excessive daytime sleepiness disorder is a condition where a person feels very tired during the day, even if they have had enough sleep at night. This can make it hard to pay attention, learn, and do daily activities.

Causes:

Sleep disorders like sleep apnea or narcolepsy

Poor sleep habits

Certain medications

Treatment:

Lifestyle Changes:

Going to bed and waking up at the same time every day

Avoiding caffeine and electronic devices before bedtime

Creating a comfortable sleep environment

Medications:

Sometimes doctors prescribe medication to help stay awake during the day.
