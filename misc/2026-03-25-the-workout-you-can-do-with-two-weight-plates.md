# The Workout You Can Do With Two Weight Plates

If the only time you pick up a weight plate is to put it on a barbell, you’re doing it wrong, says Men’s Health Fitness Director BJ Gaddour.

Weight plates are fantastic strength tools in and of themselves. Just check out Gaddour’s workout—done with two plates and a pinch grip—shown in the video above.

How to Do the Plate Pinch Workout

Perform each of the following exercises in a row, resting 1 minute between each move. Finish all 3 movements to complete 1 round. Do 3 to 5 total rounds.

1. Plate pinch row, max reps 2. Plate pinch overhead press, max reps 3. Plate pinch carry, max time/distance

Doing rows and carries this way increases your grip strength, builds your forearm muscles, and still hits all the muscles you would with barbells, dumbbells, or kettlebells, Gaddour says.
