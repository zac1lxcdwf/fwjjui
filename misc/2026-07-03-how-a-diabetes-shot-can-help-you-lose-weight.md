What is Mounjaro, and how does it help you lose weight?

Though the drug was originally only used to help patients with diabetes, a new study shows that Mounjaro has the potential to help non-diabetes patients to lose weight and keep the weight off1.

During the study, participants were given three different dosages of the medication. They showed significant weight loss, with the amount of weight loss increasing with the dosage amount compared to the placebo group. All participants had BMIs over 30, and the median weight loss was around 18% for the trial. Similar results in other trials have played a large part in why the drug is now being prescribed as a standalone medication for weight management.

Maximizing the effects of Mounjaro with medical weight loss

To understand how to maximize the results of Mounjaro treatment, it’s important to understand how medical weight loss works.

Medical weight loss is not just about losing weight by taking medication; medical weight loss is about losing weight and controlling weight gain with the help of a medical professional.

Medical weight loss does not refer to surgeries or procedures that remove body fat from the body, such as liposuction. This type of procedure is considered a cosmetic treatment. Medical weight loss is concerned with programs and treatments that help the body naturally lose weight, not artificially removing fat from the body.

All medical weight loss begins in a doctor’s office. There, the doctor will discuss with you the state of your health, any issues that you may have preventing you from losing weight and keeping it off, and how best to implement changes to improve your ability to lose and keep it off. Working with a weight loss professional will improve your diet, switch to healthier foods, lower your overall calorie intake, and adjust what you eat based on your health needs.

Behavior modification is another significant part of the weight loss process. The goal is to change negative behaviors, such as overeating at meal times or stress eating, into positive ones that improve your health. This typically involves identifying problem behaviors and learning new coping mechanisms to deal with day-to-day stresses and other triggers that might otherwise lead to poor health choices.

It’s also important to implement regular exercise. The amount and type of exercise you get will be determined by your weight loss professional and your health and fitness goals.

A medical weight loss professional may also work with you to find ways to change your environment. Sometimes, your eating and lifestyle choices are impacted by the people and places in your daily life. You can improve your lifestyle and habits by being aware of these impacts and making adjustments where necessary.

Prescription weight loss medication may also be used to support your weight loss journey. These medications are not meant to be the core of your weight loss program; they are meant to support what you are already doing under the direction of a weight loss professional. By combining all of these different aspects of medical weight loss with Mounjaro treatment, you can maximize the results of the medication, which will improve your overall weight loss and help you maintain your desired results.
