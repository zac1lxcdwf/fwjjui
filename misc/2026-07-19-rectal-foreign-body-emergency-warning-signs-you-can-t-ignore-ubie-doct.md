When is a rectal foreign body considered a medical emergency?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

A rectal foreign body becomes a medical emergency when it causes severe pain, heavy bleeding, difficulty removing the object, or signs of infection such as fever, chills, or abdominal swelling. Prompt medical care is essential to prevent dangerous complications like bowel perforation, internal bleeding, or sepsis.
