# This Mini-Workout Will Blast Your Abs, Shoulders, and Arms All At Once

Andy Speer, the creator of The Anarchy Abs Workout for Men's Health, knows a little something about how to build a rock-hard midsection. That's why this pushup and row finisher is going to scorch your abs and shoulders.
