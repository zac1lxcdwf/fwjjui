# Stop Ruining Your Sleep: The Critical Diet & Nutrition Link | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Do diet and nutrition influence sleep disorders?

[Diet Nutrition](https://taihpeptide.com/)

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Diet and nutrition can significantly affect sleep disorders. What you eat, when you eat, and how much you eat all influence sleep quality, duration, and the likelihood of conditions like insomnia, sleep apnea, and restless legs syndrome.

Key ways diet impacts sleep include:

Caffeine and alcohol: Both can disrupt sleep cycles and reduce deep sleep.

Heavy or spicy meals: Eating late can trigger acid reflux and disturb rest.

Sugar and refined carbs: May cause blood sugar swings that wake you at night.

Nutrient deficiencies: Low magnesium, iron, vitamin D, or B vitamins are linked to poor sleep and restless legs syndrome.

Sleep-supporting foods: Foods rich in tryptophan, melatonin, and complex carbs (like nuts, cherries, oats, and turkey) may promote better sleep.

Explanation

Diet and nutrition can play an important role in how well we sleep. Certain foods and nutrients can either help or hurt our sleep quality.

Healthy Diets and Sleep: Eating a balanced diet with plenty of fruits, vegetables, and whole grains can improve sleep quality. These foods contain vitamins and minerals that help our bodies relax and prepare for sleep.

Foods to Avoid: Eating too much sugar or drinking a lot of caffeine, especially close to bedtime, can make it hard to sleep. These can keep your brain active when it should be winding down.

Nutrients that Help: Some nutrients, like magnesium and tryptophan, are known to help with sleep. These can be found in foods like nuts, seeds, and turkey.
