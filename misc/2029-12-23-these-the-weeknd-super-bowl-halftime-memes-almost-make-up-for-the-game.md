# These The Weeknd Super Bowl Halftime Memes (Almost) Make Up for the Game

Super Bowl LV was considerably less hype than what the hype had hyped. Patrick Mahomes and Tom Brady were to square off like Michael Jordan and LeBron James. There was supposed to be fireworks. We were promised fireworks, damnit.

Instead, the game turned into a line-of-scrimmage cage match, where the Bucs, taking advantage of injuries on the Chiefs' offensive line, bull-rushed Mahomes all game, forcing him to throw underneath and effectively eliminating any long ball to Tyreek Hill. It was the first time Mahomes lost by double digits in an NFL game ever.

And so, in a game without explosiveness, it was the Halftime Show that shouldered all the hype. The Weeknd had put millions into the performance, which turned into what we can only imagine his After Hours world tour might have resembled had the world not pressed “pause” last March.

The show began atop the bleachers in Raymond James Stadium. It fled into a hall of mirrors. It then exploded out onto the field with an army of backup dancers, dressed in jackets and bandages. Since most Halftime shows take place on the field, there’s usually not a lot of room to work the turf. The Weeknd, however, pulled off a feat of both extravagance and appropriate social distancing and face protection—all while filling the entire field with an album performance. If only the second half could have kept the hype alive.
