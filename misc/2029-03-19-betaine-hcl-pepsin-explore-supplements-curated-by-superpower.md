Why We Chose This

Stomach acid does heavy lifting beyond digestion — it activates pepsin to break down protein, sterilizes food against pathogens, and enables absorption of B12, iron, and minerals like calcium and magnesium. Yet acid production naturally drops with age, and overuse of antacids can suppress it further, leaving you with poor protein digestion and nutrient gaps. Betaine HCl & pepsin directly addresses this by replenishing both the acid environment and the enzyme needed to process protein, helping restore the digestive cascade that supports both comfort and nutrient uptake.
