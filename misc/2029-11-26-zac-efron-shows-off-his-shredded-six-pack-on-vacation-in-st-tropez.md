# Zac Efron Shows Off His Shredded Six-Pack on Vacation in St. Tropez

Zac Efron famously got into the most shredded shape of his life for the 2017 comedy remake Baywatch, in which he played a lean, ripped Olympic athlete-turned-super-lifeguard. Efron has since gone on the record about just how physically and emotionally grueling the process of preparing his body for that movie was, acknowledging the unrealistic standard it sets.

"That Baywatch look, I don’t know if that’s really attainable," he told Men's Health in 2022. "There’s just too little water in the skin. Like, it’s fake; it looks CGI’d. And that required Lasix, powerful diuretics, to achieve. So I don’t need to do that. I much prefer to have an extra, you know, 2 to 3 percent body fat."

"I started to develop insomnia, and I fell into a pretty bad depression, for a long time," he added. "Something about that experience burned me out. I had a really hard time re-centering."

While he has stated on more than one occasion that he does not wish to ever get that lean again, Efron has once again undergone a dramatic physical transformation for his next movie The Iron Claw. He plays professional wrestler Kevin Von Erich, a.k.a. the "Golden Warrior," a member of the real-life Von Erich wrestling dynasty, and has trained to build a fighter's body for the upcoming A24 film.
