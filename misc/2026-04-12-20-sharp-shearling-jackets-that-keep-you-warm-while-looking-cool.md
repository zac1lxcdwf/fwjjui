HUMANS HAVE been wearing shearling for thousands of years. What makes shearling unique from leather is that the fur from the animal is still attached. Back in our hunter-gatherer days, we figured out that leaving the fur attached to animal hides kept us warmer. That extra heat has always been the functional appeal of shearling, but if we’re being honest, we wear it today because it looks so damn cool.

Shearling jackets have been a staple of men’s outerwear since WWII. The shearling bomber jackets that were issued to pilots during the war quickly made their way into popular fashion after the war. They were warm, durable, weather-resistant, and—most importantly—they looked damn cool. Designers have created many more forms of shearling jackets in the past few decades, but the appeal is the same: a combo of leathers and fur that appeals to our primal instincts.

We should clarify what we mean by a shearling jacket, because the definition can be vague. Some people say that a jacket has to be made of 100% animal skin to be called shearling, but we’re here for any jacket that features any shearling details. So whether it's just a shearling collar, or the whole jacket lining, it’s all shearling. We’re also including faux shearling on this list for guys who prefer not to wear animal skins, or just want an affordable alternative to the real thing.
