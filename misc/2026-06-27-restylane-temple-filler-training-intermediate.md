# Restylane Temple Filler Training | Intermediate

Preview

About this lesson

Temple filler is essential for restoring the continuity between the forehead and the mid-face, especially in patients with visible orbital rim projection, hollow temples, and loss of lateral support. In this intermediate lesson, Dr. Juan Sopena demonstrates safe and effective deep-fossa temple augmentation using Restylane® Volyme, a high–particle-size filler ideal for structural volumization.

You will learn how to assess temple depth, identify visible bone landmarks, and understand vascular considerations. This lesson shows how restoring volume in the temple region rebalances the upper face, improves eyebrow support, and enhances the harmony between the forehead and cheekbone areas.

Ideal for practitioners who want to refine temple contouring techniques for natural, smooth upper-face transitions and improved lateral brow support.

‍

Things you’ll learn

How to identify temple hollowing and assess depth using bone visibility and contour disruption.

Deep supraperiosteal placement for structural volumization in the temporal fossa.

Safe technique considerations around the superficial temporal artery and venous network.

How restoring temple volume improves forehead curvature and brow support.

Why Restylane® Volyme is appropriate for deep temple volumization due to its projection capacity.
