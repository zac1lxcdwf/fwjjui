# Take a Dive With This Badass Watch From Victorinox

So you need a new watch. Victorinox's I.N.O.X. Professional Diver is a sporty, but refined timepiece that you can wear to the boardroom or on a dive. It can serve as both a fashion accessory and a sporting tool, and you can choose from a variety of styles including a classic black dial to a bolder yellow design.

The I.N.O.X. line was designed to be nearly indestructible. (Really, watch this tank run over one.) And the Professional Diver, meant to evoke the same ruggedness as Victorinox's Swiss Army knives, has you covered for whatever underwater adventures you might embark on. It features a stainless steel bracelet and a sturdy 45-millimeter case that makes the watch stand out on any wrist demanding to be seen and noticed. Want to wear it with a tailored suit? Go ahead. Jeans? That works, too. If you're planning a dive, an extendable, folding buckle allows you to fit the watch comfortably over a diving suit. The watch's quartz movement means you'll be more than able to keep track of how long you've been underwater—or at least will help you get to a meeting on time.
