About this lesson

This lesson focuses on a male patient case with a tired look and provides step-by-step guidance on gender-specific anatomy, patient assessment, and mid-face filler treatment planning and principles for natural facial rejuvenation.

The session centers on assessment and planning, and dermal filler techniques are discussed but not demonstrated.
