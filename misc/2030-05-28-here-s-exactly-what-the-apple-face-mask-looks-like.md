It seems that nearly every company has turned to making masks in 2020, including Apple. Although they're better known for their phones and computers, the tech giant started making some impressive face coverings for their in-store employees, as YouTuber Lewis George Hilsenteger of Unbox Therapy revealed in a new video.

"This has to be one of the most unusual Apple unboxing videos I've ever done," says Hilsenteger.

In true Apple form, the masks come sealed in the company's white, minimalist packaging. Each box contains five reusable coverings and includes small clips that help you better secure the masks behind your ears. The masks should be washed after eight hours of usage and can be worn five times before being discarded.

According to Hilsenteger, Apple claims its mask is superior to standard surgical masks. But what does he think?
