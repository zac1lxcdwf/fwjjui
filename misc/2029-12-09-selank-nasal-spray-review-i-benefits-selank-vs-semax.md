What is Selank?

Selank is a synthetic peptide, which works as an adrenocorticotropic/melanocyte-stimulating hormone (ACTH-MSH). It is analogous to the endogenously occurring tetrapeptide called Tuftsin (1).

The amino acid sequence of Tuftsin is, Thr-Lys-Pro-Arg and is predominantly found in the IgG class of antibodies (2). The structure of Selank is an elongated version of Tuftsin with 3 additional amino acids, Pro-Gly-Pro (3). This adds to the stability of the molecule along with a longer half-life.

This relatively small peptide was originally designed and developed in Russia at the Institute of Molecular Genetics. Since then, scientists have constantly been researching Selank to explore its prospective benefits.

How Does Selank Work?

Selank is one of the regulatory peptides, and it modulates different reactions in the body.

Because Selank is well-known for treating symptoms related to anxiety and depression, it was believed that Selank directly influences GABA receptors and the subsequent binding of GABA. But the clinical trials suggest that Selank rather acts as an allosteric effector of GABA receptors (4).

Some evidence also suggests that Selank triggers the metabolism of neurotransmitters, such as serotonin and dopamine (5). Thus, Selank modifies the genes expressing the neuro-transmitting properties in the neuronal cells.

Selank is also known to promote the expression of Brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF).

As Selank is analogous to Tuftsin, which plays a key structural role in the IgG antibodies, it is quite self-explanatory that Selank modulates many immunological reactions.

Selank regulates the expression of factors, such as Interleukin-6 (IL-6) (6). This subsequently influences the immunocompetent cells, such as T-helper cytokines.
