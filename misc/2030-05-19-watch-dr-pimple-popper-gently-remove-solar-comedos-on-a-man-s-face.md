Dr. Pimple Popper gets into ASMR territory in a chill #throwbackthursday video posted to Instagram today. With the gentlest push from her comedone extractor, dermatologist and TLC host Dr. Sandra Lee, MD, managers to make a man’s solar comedos sprout out buds of white goop.
