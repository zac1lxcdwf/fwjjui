John Singleton died on April 29 at the age of 51, and friends and fans alike are sharing their memories of the director online. Singleton directed movies like Boyz n the Hood, Poetic Justice, 2 Fast 2 Furious, and Four Brothers. In 1992, at the age of 24, he was nominated for an Academy Award for Boyz n the Hood, making him the youngest person ever nominated for Best Director, and the first African-American to be nominated for the award.

"He broke open the door for black directors and paved a way we are still building on," Keith Powell tweeted.
