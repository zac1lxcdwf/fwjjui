# Blast Your Upper Back

How do top trainers come up with the crazy new exercises we bring to you in our Exercise of the Week newsletter? There are many methods, but sometimes it’s pretty simple: They get bored.

That’s what happened to Jeremy Frisch, U.S.A.W, creator of the 24-Hour Arms workout, with his athletes and the inverted row.

[Your Upper Back](https://mendstride.snaketongueofficial.com/)

[Your Upper Back](https://collagen-atelier.bbsxyxy.com/blog/6029034060.html)

The inverted row is a favorite exercise among fitness experts because it strengthens the often-neglected muscles in your rear shoulders and upper back. “But after a while, we were looking for something to change it up, because you just get sick of doing the same thing,” Frisch says.

Frisch’s upgrade? The side-to-side inverted row. Watch the video below to see how to do it.

Just like a traditional inverted row, the side-to-side inverted row works your upper back and rear deltoids—but it adds a new challenge that cranks up the intensity. “When our athletes who can do the inverted row starting doing the side-to-side version, it was a new challenge,” Frisch says. “They really had to work hard to achieve the full range of motion.”
