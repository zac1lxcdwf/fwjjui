1. What Are Peptides?

Definition: Peptides are short chains of two to 50 amino acids (the building blocks of proteins).

Role: They carry signals between cells and tissues, influencing processes like growth, repair, metabolism, and hormone release.

Why they matter: Unlike full proteins, peptides can be more targeted, easier to absorb, and quicker acting.

2. Why Peptides for Men?

Men produce various hormones and growth factors that decline with age. This can lead to:

Decreased muscle mass and strength

Slower recovery from workouts or injury

Lower libido and erectile function

Reduced energy and mood changes

Thinning skin and slower wound healing

Peptides for men aim to boost or optimize these natural processes by mimicking or stimulating your body's own signaling molecules.
