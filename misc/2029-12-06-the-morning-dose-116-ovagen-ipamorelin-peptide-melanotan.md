Photo by Alora Griffiths on Unsplash

Ovagen is a bioregulatory peptide studied for its ability to protect and regenerate liver tissue at the gene expression level. In 2025, it’s gaining traction in peptide stacks aimed at detox, post-cycle recovery, and anti-aging protocols.

What’s Covered:

🛡️ Mechanisms of ActionOvagen targets hepatocyte DNA, promoting healthy liver cell function and accelerating tissue recovery under oxidative or toxic stress. It’s part of the Khavinson peptide family focused on organ-specific repair.

🛡️ Benefits for Liver HealthResearch links Ovagen to improved liver enzyme profiles, detoxification efficiency, and resilience against pharmaceutical or alcohol-related liver strain. It may also support metabolic stability and immune response.
