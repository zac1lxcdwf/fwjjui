What is a Muscle Spasm and Cramp?

A muscle spasm, cramp, or a Charlie horse is a sudden, unmistakable tightening or contracting of one of your muscles. Cramps and spasms are involuntary and are most common in the legs, specifically the calves, feet, hamstrings, thighs, and hips.

While muscle cramps and spasms can happen anytime, anywhere, they most commonly occur during exercise or physical activities where you’re using energy and putting stress on a specific group of muscles.

Symptoms of Muscle Cramps and Spasms

Muscle cramps and spasms are unmistakable, regardless of which muscle they affect.

Here are some of the symptoms you may experience during an attack:

Sudden tightness

Extreme contraction

Feeling like someone punched or kneed you in the affected muscle

Inability to move or stretch the muscle

Twitching under your skin due to muscle contraction

The muscle tissue is dense and hard
