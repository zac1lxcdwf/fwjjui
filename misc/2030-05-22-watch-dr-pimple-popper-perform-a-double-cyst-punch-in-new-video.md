In the video—which turns out to be a twofer, involving a pair of separate cyst removals—Dr. Lee uses her punch tool to create a perfectly round opening for extracting and squeezing out the cyst. As she explains to the in-house audience of "popaholics," as she refers to her most hardcore followers, "Now that I've opened the area and squeezed out most of the cyst, I'm just trying to remove the sac wall." Removing the sac wall is common when treating a sebaceous cyst to help ensure that it won't simply regrow following the procedure.

After using her tweezers to remove the sac—and, in the first case, grappling with what may be a second cyst wall growing under the first one—Dr. Lee stitches the satisfied patient back up, and even takes a moment to give his family an up close and personal look at the results of the procedure, including the gunk she squeezed out.
