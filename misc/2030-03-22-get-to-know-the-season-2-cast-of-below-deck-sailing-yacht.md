# Get to Know the Season 2 Cast of 'Below Deck Sailing Yacht'

Get ready to set sail—after a polarizing first season, Below Deck: Sailing Yacht is coming back yet again for Season 2 on Monday, March 1. And while Sailing's favorite captain Glenn Shephard is returning to the Mediterranean with his trusty boat Parsifal III, this time, he's bringing with him a whole new crew.

[the Season Cast of](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/blog/4839133872.html)

This season, we'll be introduced to several yachtie amateurs and experienced professionals alike, including one stew who comes from a family of yachties and a chef who worked in the kitchens of Michelin-starred restaurants. But before the wild season premieres, let's get to know the charter crew of Parsifal III, ready to make a splash on a television screen near you.

"Below Deck: Sailing Yacht" is back for a second season starting March 1st at 9PM ET.
