# Derek Hough Shows Off Shredded Six-Pack in Instagram Thirst Trap

[Hough Shows](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/blog/6809952810.html)

In the world of ballroom dancing, the outfits are often as eye-catching as the moves—at least, until the music stops. In a thirst trap on Instagram, Dancing With the Stars mainstay Derek Hough, who often posts "day in the life" videos to social media, has shared a look at what his "post show outfit" entails, showing off his shredded dancer's physique and defined six-pack while wearing nothing but a towel and a baseball cap.

In addition to dance, Hough works out five times a week to stay lean and sculpt those abs, incorporating a wide range of exercises into his strength and conditioning routine including weights training, boxing, swimming, outdoor biking, mixed martial arts, and yoga.

In a 2020 interview with Men's Health, Hough explained that during his stint as one of the professionals on Dancing With the Stars—during which he secured a record-breaking six wins, not to mention three Emmys—his top priority when working out was building the kind of endurance needed to prep for the live shows.

"[It's] the eighth round of a 12-hour day and it’s the final dance and I'm dying. All your technique and all your hours of training go out the window and you're just surviving," he said. "And that's a terrible feeling. It's a terrible feeling when you’ve worked so hard and all this technique and nuance goes out the window when you lose that energy. So for me it's about energy."

Of course, these days Hough is in a slightly more comfortable position behind the desk with the rest of the judging panel. But as his latest thirst trap proves, he's still keeping on moving.
