How can I manage frequent mood swings effectively?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Frequent mood swings may signal underlying conditions such as panic disorder, anxiety disorders, hormonal imbalances, or early signs of bipolar disorder. Identifying the root cause is key to managing symptoms effectively and improving daily well-being.

Because mood swings can stem from many different causes—some medical, some psychological, and some lifestyle-related—understanding what's driving yours is the essential first step. A free, instant, online symptom check can help you clarify possible causes and confidently navigate your next steps.
