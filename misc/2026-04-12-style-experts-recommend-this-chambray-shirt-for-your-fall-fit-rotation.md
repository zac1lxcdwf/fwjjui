What to Consider

Locking down a good chambray shirt is not as complex as buying a car, but there are some considerations to take note of.

Material

What goes into a chambray shirt is a major factor, as it determines the feel and weight as well as how you might wash the shirt. Some materials offer more of a worn-in look and fade more over time while others be firmer to accentuate the fit. On top of that, it simply pays to know what you're wearing and how that fabric came to be (paying attention to pluses like organic cotton, of course).

Pockets

The pockets can make or break a chambray shirt. Too over-the-top and the western flair gets a little out of control and you can only wear the thing line dancing. Too subtle and the pocket may provide little to no use, let alone offer any stylistic bonus points. Pockets are generally a must, however we did land on one shirt that was good enough even without the signature accent.

Colors

Sure, iconic blue is great but it's 2024. You should have a good color option or two, or at least some variation in wash and stitching so that two blue options offer a different look.

Stitching Details

An intricate detail like stitching can separate your chambray shirt from the pack. Fashion is all about these little things and we left no seam unchecked. From clean and practically unnoticeable to loud and proud, the stitching almost functions like a pattern on the shirt and also determines how the thing will fit around your arms, torso, and the like.

How We Selected

Our fashion team, including Deputy Commerce Editor Christian Gollayan, put dozens of chambray shirts to the test, wearing them in all kinds of functions. We donned them to work, assessed their breathability and comfort level while commuting, and see how they caught the eye and happy hour functions and dinner gatherings.

To whittle down the list to just the half-dozen best, we gauged not only the considerations above, but how these garments handled varying weather conditions, how well they paired with other clothing, and additional accents like button makeup, pocket shape, sleeve length and more.

Our team came away with some chambray shirts that do more than just look great. They fit like a dream, feature well in casual and formal settings alike, and offer a bit more than your run-of-the-mill button down piece. Naturally, part of the testing process involved wearing a chambray in the many ways its intended to be worn―tucked and untucked, sleeves down and sleeves rolled up, with shorts for a stroll in the park and with jeans for a meal out.
