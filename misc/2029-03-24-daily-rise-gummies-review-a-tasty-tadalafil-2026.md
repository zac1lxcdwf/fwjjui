Effectiveness

Tadalafil is the main ingredient in Daily Rise Gummies, and numerous studies show that it’s effective as a daily medication to treat ED.1 Taken every day, typically at doses of 2.5mg or 5mg, tadalafil reliably allows men to achieve erections hard enough for penetrative sex.2 And, as a daily medication with a window of efficacy up to 36 hours, it’s more or less effective on command once that initial dose is active in your system.

Daily Rise delivers 7mg in each of its gummies, which ought to be plenty for most men who would respond well to 2.5mg or 5mg in a tablet. That makes Daily Rise reliably effective for most men. And the gummy format may provide better bioavailability than traditional tablets, with a bioequivalence study in vitamin D significantly favoring gummies over tablets and capsules for maximum blood concentration.3

As-needed tadalafil in pill form is also sometimes prescribed at doses as low as 5mg, so the 7mg in Daily Rise might even be enough for some men to take on an as-needed basis, instead.2 And research indicates a phenomenon called linear pharmacokinetics for tadalafil, which is a fancy way of saying that higher doses tend to yield improved outcomes.4 So, that 7mg should also be more effective than 5mg on a daily basis.

With that in mind, men interested in taking tadalafil on an as-needed basis could invest in Daily Rise Gummies and only take them in the vicinity of intercourse. They could also theoretically double their dose and take two gummies if using them as needed, but that shouldn’t be done without first consulting your doctor.

How do Daily Rise Gummies work?

Daily Rise Gummies work thanks to a single drug contained in each dose called tadalafil. Tadalafil is the generic form of Cialis, a popular ED drug that differentiates itself from sildenafil (generic for Viagra) thanks to its longer duration of action. Whereas sildenafil lasts for about six hours after you take it, tadalafil can last for up to 36 hours.6 2

Of course, those timetables are examples of as-needed use. Another differentiator between sildenafil and tadalafil is that you can take a lower dose of tadalafil daily and be ready for intercourse whenever the moment strikes. That means there’s no waiting for the medication to kick in or concern that you might not have it nearby when you need it.

Tadalafil is part of a class of drugs called phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE5) inhibitors.7 And PDE5 plays a pretty simple role in erectile health. Essentially, PDE5 manages the concentration of a cellular messenger in your blood called cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP). When cGMP builds up, say, in response to erotic stimuli, blood vessels in and around the penis relax, allowing chambers of the penis to fill with blood and become engorged.8 That’s an erection in a nutshell.

Insider Tip: PDE5 inhibitors don’t automatically give you an erection. That’s a misconception fueled by popular media. Instead, they create conditions in the body much more favorable to the occurrence of a healthy erection in response to stimuli. If there’s nothing there to arouse you, you won’t spontaneously get an erection.

PDE5’s job is to make sure that erection eventually goes away, typically after climax. But an imbalance in cGMP and PDE5 or signaling issues that cause PDE5 to release en masse prematurely can kill an erection either before it ever begins or right in the middle of intercourse.8 As their name implies, PDE5 inhibitors temporarily block this release, allowing cGMP to build up and creating an environment for an erection to occur in response to appropriate stimuli.

When you take Daily Rise Gummies every day, they consistently correct for any issues in that balance between cGMP and PDE5, so you’re always ready to respond to stimuli when they occur, and an erection will last until after climax.

Are Daily Rise Gummies safe?

For the most part, Daily Rise Gummies are very safe. That’s both because there’s a thorough medical intake involved before you can get a prescription and because the drugs in question have been studied so extensively. That extensive study means that nearly any potential side effect that could occur has a known mechanism and a potential treatment, whether that means titrating your dose, switching to another ED medication, or adding a complementary therapy to the picture.5

Decades of study have also codified a litany of potential contraindications for tadalafil and other PDE5 inhibitors. That makes it more likely that your consulting physician can identify a drug or condition that makes you unsuitable for treatment long before you’d ever take it and experience an adverse reaction.

Here’s a look at some of these potential side effects that have been established in the literature:

Back pain and myalgia

One of the more common side effects associated with tadalafil and affecting nearly 10% of participants in the research is back pain and myalgia.5 Often, PDE5 inhibitors inhibit other phosphodiesterase enzymes in addition to PDE5. Sildenafil, for example, has been shown to inhibit PDE6, resulting in vision disturbances as a common side effect that you don’t get from tadalafil.10 But a thorough investigation into tadalafil’s back pain side effect showed that its inhibition of PDE11 — once thought to be at the center of tadalafil’s back pain issue — was too minimal to be causative.11

Follow-up investigations of back pain therapies revealed that a simple three-day regimen of add-on medications was enough to relieve tadalafil-associated back pain and prevent its recurrence.12

Headache

While headache is a relatively common medication side effect, it can be especially pronounced in PDE5 inhibitors, affecting more than 15% of users.5 In fact, some migraine research has turned to PDE5 inhibitors as an instigator to cause migraines in clinical settings.13 Unfortunately, the research picture for men with migraine is a little muddy, though, as a recent study in men with migraine without aura saw PDE5 inhibitors cause headaches but not migraines.14

Ultimately, headache is a side effect best treated with titration and NSAIDs, or, eventually, trying a different PDE5 inhibitor.

Dyspepsia

Dyspepsia encompasses things like chest pain, heartburn, and nausea, and it occurs in nearly 12% of tadalafil users.5 While the mechanisms haven’t been established conclusively, a systematic review of studies in patients with esophageal motility disorders found that PDE5 inhibitors could alleviate their symptoms by, among other things, reducing the resting pressure of the lower esophageal sphincter (LES, the muscle that closes off your esophagus from your stomach).15

That’s good news if you have hypertensive LES that makes it difficult to swallow or pass food into your stomach. However, in healthy individuals, a relaxation of the LES could result in dyspepsia. Currently, treatment would involve acute medications and some lifestyle changes to manage acidity, but men with a significant negative dyspeptic reaction may need to find alternatives to PDE5 inhibitors for ED treatment.

Nasopharyngitis

Nasopharyngitis, or a swelling of the nasal passages, is essentially just nasal congestion. It appears in a little over 10% of users, but it’s typically manageable with over-the-counter interventions.5

Alternatives to Daily Rise

With the incredible abundance of ED treatment options out there, it’s imperative to examine all of your options before making a decision about where to start. Daily Rise Gummies might be the perfect fit for you, but if they aren’t, you’ll still have plenty of options to explore.

We dive deep into these options in our guide to the best ED treatments, but let’s take a moment here to go over some highlights that could get you moving in the right direction:

Other novel daily tadalafil sources

We’ve discussed at length throughout this piece that there are a couple of viable options for men determined to take their daily tadalafil in a format other than traditional tablets. The two most prominent on the market are BlueChew and Hims, each of which offer some unique advantages over Roman.

In BlueChew’s case, a quarterly subscription costs a hair less than Roman, and even though we prefer the flavor of Daily Rise Gummies, some men might like BlueChew’s berry flavor a bit better. BlueChew also offers a slightly stronger 9mg dose. In Hims’ case, prices are much lower, and you get two dose options: 8.5mg and 11.2mg.

Traditional tadalafil tablets

If you want dose flexibility that allows you to start treatment at the lowest available dose and titrate up until you see benefits (the safest way to approach a new medication), tablets are the way to go. Typical tadalafil tablet prescriptions start at just 2.5mg/day, less than half the fixed 7mg dose from Roman.

Tablets also have the advantage of being the least expensive way to get your hands on daily tadalafil, with subscriptions from Hims and a couple of other companies delivering tadalafil for just over $1 per day.

Other PDE5 inhibitors

Whether the daily approach isn’t for you, or you experience some side effects with tadalafil that might not be as pronounced with other PDE5 inhibitors, reaching for something like sildenafil or vardenafil (generic for Levitra) could be the right move. Daily tadalafil tends to offer the lowest cost per dose, but if you aren’t having sex more than a few times per month, you could save money with an as-needed medication.

Mental health care

While psychological and physiological aspects of an ED diagnosis are often intertwined, research suggests that up to 40% of ED cases are psychogenic in origin, meaning that they began with mental health.16 Seeking mental health treatment in these cases may be enough to reverse your ED, and you can rely on medications to help maintain your sex life while you work on more complex underlying mental health concerns.

Combination therapies

Several companies, including Roman, Hims, BlueChew, and Rugiet, have seen fit to combine two or more ED medications into a single therapy, often delivered in some kind of novel format, like a chewable tablet or dissolvable lozenge. These typically aren’t the best places for men new to ED meds to start, as increasing the number of medications in your regimen could lead to increased side effect risk.

Roman’s version of a combination therapy is called Ro Sparks, and it puts 22mg of tadalafil and 55mg of sildenafil in a single dissolving lozenge. Those are some relatively strong doses, though Sparks is intended for as-needed use only, as are most combination therapies. Sparks is also one of the most expensive combination therapies on the market, with only Rugiet outpacing it for price.
