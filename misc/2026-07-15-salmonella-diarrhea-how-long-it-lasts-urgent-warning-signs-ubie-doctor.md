# Salmonella Diarrhea: How Long It Lasts & Urgent Warning Signs | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/15/2026

How long does diarrhea typically last in a salmonella infection, and when should you seek medical attention?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Salmonella-related diarrhea typically lasts 4 to 7 days and often resolves on its own without treatment. However, you should seek medical attention if diarrhea is severe, persists longer than a week, or is accompanied by warning signs such as a high fever (over 102°F), bloody stools, persistent vomiting, or signs of dehydration like dizziness, dry mouth, or reduced urination. Infants, older adults, pregnant women, and people with weakened immune systems face higher risks of complications and should consult a doctor sooner.
