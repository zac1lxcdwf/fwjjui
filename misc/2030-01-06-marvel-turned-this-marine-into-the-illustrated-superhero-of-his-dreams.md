# Marvel Turned This Marine Into the Illustrated Superhero of His Dreams

Marvel created some of the biggest fictional superheroes of all time: Spider-Man, Iron Man, Thor, Captain America, The Hulk. But its latest hero is based in reality.

[the Illustrated Superhero of](https://calmvector.dns0663.com/blog/6065884249.html)

Marvel recently teamed up with the Wounded Warrior Project to "celebrate our real-life heroes" by turning service members into illustrated Marvel superheroes. And a veteran Marine named Miki is one of the lucky few to get the Marvel treatment.

The Costa Rica-native describes himself as a husband, father, and Marine. But he's also extremely humble. Miki is also a Purple Heart recipient, calling it "the medal nobody wants."

"I got a Purple Heart by being blown up by multiple IEDs, but one that banged me up pretty good," Miki says in a video by Marvel. "[I] had some traumatic brain injuries, but luckily I'm still able to carry on complete sentences, and still walk and run."

When it comes to capturing his essence as a Marvel hero, Miki says his hero would be a loyal man of integrity who never leaves a man behind. "Some people run away from gunfire. I run straight towards it,” Miki says. “If there is an issue going on, I’ll get there fast.”

In fact, Miki says running would be a big part of his superhero power, as he is an avid runner and a big supporter of the Wounded Warrior Project via the nonprofit's Carry Forward 5K runs.

"I just found it as a way of stress relief," Miki says of running. "Just get out there, outdoors, and you just kind of zone out. Put on some tunes and just get after it."

At the end of the video, Miki sees his illustrated Marvel persona for the first time—and it has all the elements that make Miki a real-life superhero. "Woah, that’s pretty cool," he says in reaction. "That’s awesome."
