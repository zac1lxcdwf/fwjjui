What is gastritis and what causes it?

Gastritis is the inflammation of the stomach’s protective lining. Acute gastritis is a short-term condition, whereas chronic gastritis can linger for an extended period of time. When to seek professional help for gastritis symptoms

It can seem confusing to know when you should seek medical help with your gastritis symptoms. Some signs that you should consult a professional include:

Lingering symptoms despite making recommended lifestyle changes

Chronic pain in the upper belly/abdomen

Vomiting blood, or material that looks like coffee grounds

Your doctor will ask many questions about your symptoms, family history of gastritis, and lifestyle. Your doctor may also recommend testing or procedures to check for any damage caused to your digestive system. Antibiotics may be prescribed if you test positive for H. pylori.
