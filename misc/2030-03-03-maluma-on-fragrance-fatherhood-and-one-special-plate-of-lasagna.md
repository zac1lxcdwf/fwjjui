MALUMA KNOWS HOW to make an impression. Recently named one of the ambassadors for BOSS Bottled Beyond Eau de Parfum—alongside actor/director Bradley Cooper and footballer Vinícius Júnior—the 31-year-old pop star marked the fragrance’s launch with a historic performance atop the Empire State Building’s 86th Floor Observatory on October 3rd, becoming the first artist ever to perform there before a live audience. Men’s Health caught up with Maluma before the show to talk about the scent, the role fragrance has played in his life, and one very special plate of lasagna.

For Maluma, the experience of working with Cooper and Júnior was one of the most rewarding aspects of the campaign. “It was amazing. It was fun. It was funny,” he tells Men’s Health. The night before they shot the campaign’s ad, Maluma, Cooper, and Júnior got together at Júnior’s house to become acquainted. He remembers walking in on quite a sight of the Academy Award-nominated actor and director sprawled out on the couch. “I was expecting everything, but seeing Bradley Cooper with a lot of Brazilian guys watching soccer.”

And at first, it was a bit awkward. He explains that the trio comes from three entirely different backgrounds—Maluma’s from Colombia, Júnior’s from Brazil, and Cooper’s from the States—leading to an initial cultural barrier. When I ask Maluma what broke the ice, he barely flinches. “It was the lasagna.” This favorite dish of Júnior’s acted as a universal language, and once it was served and enjoyed, the three began to vibe. “The energy was flowing. It was a very special moment that, of course, marked my life,” Maluma says.

The BOSS Bottled Beyond scent is a lot like that dinner, featuring three notes you wouldn’t think naturally fit to create something unique and powerful. Or, as Maluma describes it, “Timeless.” It opens with an energizing spicy ginger, then unfolds into smooth leather before settling with a woody oud. I find it to be ideal for evenings and cooler weather, when its depth and warmth really shine. Compared to BOSS classics like Bottled or The Scent, Beyond is more intense, mature, and longer-lasting—perfect for someone who wants a sophisticated upgrade to their familiar BOSS signatures.

Maluma says he’s been a long-time fan of BOSS fragrances, but Bottled Beyond is especially personal. He’s a huge animal lover, and the leather reminds him of his ranch in Colombia, where he has cows, goats, chickens, and horses. “When I smell this, and I smell the leather, I smell the saddles that I use to ride the horses,” Maluma says. “So, it reminds me of my hometown too.”

It also reminds him of his childhood and the man who introduced him to fragrances, his father. Maluma remembers seeing his dad apply colognes and fragrances every morning before he’d head out to work, and eventually, he’d pass this passion down. “He taught me how to do it, and it was a moment that we shared as dad and son,” he says. “To me, those moments were very, very important.”

Decades later, Maluma is nearly always rocking a fragrance. He says he wears cologne to the gym, after the gym, on tour, before he’s performing, and after he’s performing. Given that, he has amassed no shortage of options, often changing up his scent with the seasons. When he finds one he likes, he always keeps it on hand. “I have lots of fragrances. But once I like one, I fly with it everywhere,” he says. Maluma’s latest obsession? BOSS Bottled Beyond. “I have like three bottles of the BOSS Bottled Beyond here, and I already have [other bottles] in different pockets or other different places if I need some.”

A major theme of the campaign is inspiration, and where we find it. For what inspires Maluma at this stage of his career—after becoming a global superstar with multiple Grammy Award nominations, chart-topping bangers, collaborations with icons like Madonna and Shakira, and even expanding into acting with roles, such as in Marry Me alongside Jennifer Lopez—the answer is clear. “My daughter,” he says. “I'm a dad for the first time, and it changed the perspective I had about life.”

He says his daughter has fundamentally changed who he is as a person. “I'm another human being, to be honest, after my daughter was born. She inspires me to be a better human being.” Maluma explains that his primary inspiration now is to be a good example for his daughter and show her everything that the world has to offer when you open yourself to it, which requires putting in the work. “She inspires me to get better every day. Competition is not with someone else, it's just with me, and I’m proving to her that dreams come true.”

Maluma hopes to one day teach his daughter about fragrance in the way his father did for him. In fact, she’s already curious at just over 18 months. “She knows when I’m using fragrance,” he says, jokingly adding that it has led to her mother being concerned she’ll want to smell too much like her dad one day. “She loves the way I smell. She hugs me, and she doesn't want to go away.”

As for whether BOSS Bottled Beyond will still be in the rotation when that time comes, Maluma has high expectations for the scent’s longevity. “I know in 100 years, we're going to smell it, and we're going to keep liking it,” he says.
