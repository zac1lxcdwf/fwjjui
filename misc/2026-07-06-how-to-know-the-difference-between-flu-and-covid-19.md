# How To Know The Difference Between Flu And Covid-19

How To Know The Difference Between Flu And Covid-19

The flu and Covid-19 are both viruses that infect the respiratory tract, and they can be easily spread between humans through contamination from water droplets from the airway passages. They have similar symptoms: namely, respiratory depression, pneumonia, fever, and more. However, they are actually different viruses. Covid-19 is from the coronavirus family while the flu is caused by the influenza virus.
