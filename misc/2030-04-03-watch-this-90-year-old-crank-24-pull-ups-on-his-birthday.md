# ​Watch This 90-Year-Old Crank 24 Pull-Ups On His Birthday

Old people at the gym are sort of freaky, because they sometimes look like any single exercise could reduce them to a pile of flappy skin and bone powder. However, we love to be shown exceptions at Men’s Health, and as eternal endorsers of life-long fitness, seeing seasoned veterans of life hit the gym is as inspiring and encouraging as can be. Vern, a 90-year-old father from Haines City, Florida, took it to the young guys this week by repping out 24 honest-to-goodness pull-ups on his birthday. Vern’s son, who cheered him on the whole way, caught it all on camera.

Critics might point to Vern’s limited range of motion in the arms, but we’ll have you know that the regular gym-goer is restricted by a childhood incident in which he broke both his elbows. There’s no way you can knock this guy. His form is great. He doesn’t cheat. He even walks off the set like a champ. And we have to give bonus points for doing the whole thing under the gaze of his son—more evidence for the legitimacy of dad strength.

Vern reportedly goes to the gym three times a week, and here’s hoping he maintains the requisite old-man gym uniform of short-sleeve collared shirt, khaki cargo trousers, and terrific, breathable, ankle-supporting sandals. And lest we forget, Vern showed some great awareness for proper safety measures and back support—he belted up. You can watch the full video of Vern’s 24 pull-ups below, complete with his son’s delightful commentary.
