It's been a whole year since Avengers: Endgame swooped into theaters to resolve one of the biggest cliffhangers in cinematic history, and directors Joe and Anthony Russo have been marking the anniversary by sharing details from early drafts of the movie that audiences never got to see.

One of the many, many surprise reveals in Endgame revolved around Chris Hemsworth's Thor, the God of Thunder who lost his brother Loki and was unable to prevent the decimation of his people in Avengers: Infinity War. When Bruce Banner (Mark Ruffalo) catches up with Thor five years after the Snap, the thoroughly depressed King of New Asgard looks... well, a little different.

The Russo Brothers went all-in on illustrating Thor's inner struggles through a visible outer transformation, giving the usually-ripped Asgardian a headful of unwashed white man dreads and a beer gut, and showing him spending all day eating junk food and playing Fortnite with Korg. The original script, however, called for a more graphic depiction of Thor's desperate state: drunkenly taking a pee all over his kingdom.

"There was once a scene of Thor peeing off of a balcony in New Asgard to show how depraved he was," the Russos tweeted. "We realized he was already pretty depraved."

It's unclear whether this scene was actually filmed and then edited out, or if it was one of multiple moments that got cut during the writing stage. As Chris Hemsworth has previously told fans, the cast of Endgame were never given a full script during filming, and Mark Ruffalo admitted he was so prone to accidentally revealing spoilers that he was forced to shoot two endings for his character in Infinity War.

In another fun, Fortnite-related Easter egg, the Russo Brothers revealed that they cast Donald Mustard, creative director of Epic Games, as one of the sorcerers of the Sanctum Sanctorum in the movie's final battle.

While fans might have left Thor in a much less "depraved" state at the end of the movie, it's still unclear whether he'll be back to his old shredded self in the upcoming Thor: Love and Thunder, with director Taika Waititi saying the character's weight is subject of "ongoing debate."
