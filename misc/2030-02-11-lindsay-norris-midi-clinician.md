# Lindsay Norris, Midi Clinician

[Lindsay Norris Midi](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/0059539852.html)

[Lindsay Norris Midi](https://3899241.com/m98jaehiwp/)

[Norris Midi Clinician](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/)

Lindsay Norris, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

From primary care to women’s health: "After previously working in healthcare as a recreational therapist, I started in primary care in 2018. I first worked at an in-person internal medicine academic center and later in telehealth primary care. Over time, I realized how often women’s midlife concerns were overlooked or under-addressed. I wanted to change that — not just for my patients, but for the women in my own circle of friends and family."

A personal connection to the midlife journey: "As a woman in midlife myself, I understand how complex and emotional this stage can be. It’s something I talk about with the women around me all the time, and it motivates me to be a trusted resource for others navigating the same experience. Ultimately, my goal is to help every woman feel informed, supported, and empowered to take charge of her health with confidence."

Empathy and collaboration at the core: "When I meet with a patient, I start by asking what matters most to her. Together, we build a treatment plan that reflects her priorities and values. My goal is for every woman I care for to leave feeling understood, hopeful, and confident that she’s getting the care she deserves."
