# Your Weekend Challenge

Introducing the Men’s Health Weekend Challenge! Every Friday, we’ll propose one fitness test for you to attempt over the weekend. This weekend’s challenge: How many planks to pushups can you perform—with perfect form—in 2 minutes? The plank is one of the best ways to strengthen your abs. But in order to challenge your muscles in new ways, you need to progress the exercise, says Todd Durkin, C.S.C.S., trainer to many pro athletes—from football to basketball to baseball. That's why he chose the plank to pushup for this weekend's challenge. It's a much harder version of the classic exercise because it requires that you keep your core perfectly still while moving as fast as you can. The result is not only more core strength but also greater total-body stability.
