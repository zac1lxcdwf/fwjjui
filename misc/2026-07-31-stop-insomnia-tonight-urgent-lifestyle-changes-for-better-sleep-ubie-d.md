Insomnia can often be improved through simple lifestyle adjustments. Maintaining a consistent sleep schedule—going to bed and waking up at the same time daily—helps regulate your body's internal clock. Reducing stress through relaxation techniques, exercise, and mindfulness can also significantly improve sleep quality for people struggling with insomnia.

Explanation

Insomnia can be a challenging condition, but certain lifestyle changes may help improve sleep quality. According to Fleming, establishing a consistent sleep routine is crucial for managing insomnia. This means going to bed and waking up at the same time every day, even on weekends. Additionally, creating a relaxing bedtime routine, such as reading a book or taking a warm bath, can signal your body that it's time to wind down.

Another important aspect is managing stress, as highlighted in the research. Stress can significantly impact sleep, so finding ways to relax and reduce anxiety can be beneficial. Techniques such as deep breathing, meditation, or gentle yoga before bed might help calm the mind.
