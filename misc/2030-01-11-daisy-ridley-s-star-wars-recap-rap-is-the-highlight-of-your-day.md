# Daisy Ridley’s ‘Star Wars’ Recap Rap Is the Highlight of Your Day

Remembering what happened in every Star Wars movie can be tough.

With The Rise of Skywalker set to wrap up the three trilogies of 'The Skywalker Saga,' Daisy Ridley offered her best recap rap on The Tonight Show.

The actress who plays Rey showed off her MC skills, and also helped get everyone back up to speed.

[the Highlight of Your](https://pepvoice.com/)

There are a quite a few Star Wars movies—10, so far, to be exact—so remembering what, exactly, happened in all of them without devoting the lengthy time needed for a complete rewatch, can be difficult. Thankfully, with The Rise of Skywalker on the horizon, Daisy Ridley recognizes that, and took the stage on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon to deliver a worthwhile recap in the way she deemed best possible: a stirring rap.

[Highlight of Your](https://tissue-renewal.china-akan.com/)

Ridley, who plays the protagonist of the latest trilogy of Star Wars films in the mysterious Rey, doesn't quite hit every movie in her rap—she sticks to what's referred to as 'The Skywalker Saga,' so Rogue One and Solo don't make it here.

Still, it's an admirable job to recap so much story, as Fallon stands by with art on cards, and The Roots play a fun variation on John Williams' iconic Star Wars theme, the Imperial March, and the Cantina Bar theme. Daisy's ad-libs aren't too bad either; we get a Gucci Mane-esque"brr" when discussing The Empire Strikes Back's snow planet, Hoth.

[Ridley Star](https://cellular.wiki/trlu0p/)

Ridley's flow switches up a few times, and it's a pretty impressive rap performance, overall. Fun stuff, no question. But when it comes to recapping Star Wars movies, it's always going to be hard to top Weird Al Yankovic's "The Saga Begins," sung to the tune of Don McLean's "American Pie" and recapping the entire first prequel film, The Phantom Menace.

We're less than a month out from The Rise of Skywalker's release, so the time to start hammering out that series has certainly snuck up. If not, this rap should probably do the trick.
