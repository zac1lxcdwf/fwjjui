What to Consider

Design

Workout mirrors are either freestanding or wall-mounted. Freestanding models offer more flexibility, as they can be moved wherever and whenever you need them. However, apartment dwellers and anyone with limited square footage should note that they do take up more floor space.

Wall-mounted workout mirrors provide a sleeker and more integrated aesthetic with your existing home decor. But because they're typically heavy, some require special brackets to mount them securely and safely to your wall. This can add a few hundred dollars to the already pricey cost of a mirror and, depending on your level of DIY comfort, an extra fee to have it professionally installed.

Subscriptions and Fees

The beauty of today's best fitness workout mirrors lies in their guided workouts. Look for mirrors with a deep class library that fits your needs, whether that's casual yoga, all-out strength training, HIIT, or some unique combination. All fitness mirrors require ongoing (usually monthly) subscription fees, so you'll also want to factor that cost into the long-term price.

Durability

You want a workout mirror that's durable enough to keep up with your workout regimen. Look for models with tempered glass and a sturdy frame, especially if you tend to go hard in the gym. All of our recommendations below feature extremely durable designs.

Dimensions and Weight

All of the workout mirrors we recommend weigh between 100 and 150 pounds. Most measure roughly the same size: About six feet tall by two feet wide. Consider these overall dimensions when determining how much floor space you'll need to set up your new mirror, and remember to allow enough space on all sides to move around freely and safely. If you intend to wall-mount yours, you'll also need to pay careful attention to the weight and mounting requirements.

How Do Workout Mirrors Work?

True to their name, workout mirrors are specially designed to allow you to watch a fitness instructor or certified trainer while simultaneously watching yourself as you workout. This unique setup makes it easy to check your form and emulate your onscreen coach. Depending on the exact mirror model, you'll also have access to essential metrics—time elapsed, calories burned, heart rate, etc.—throughout your workouts.

Are Workout Mirrors Worth It?

The short answer is: It depends. Workout mirrors are a clever, high-tech niche product designed for a specific type of fitness enthusiast. They're great for anyone who is unable (or unwilling) to go to the gym or who might be uncomfortable sharing their workout time with others in a class setting. They create a more personal experience that feels like "your" trainer is working just with you in a virtual one-on-one setting. The best fitness workout mirrors also provide instant access to key metrics while you workout.

How Much Space Do You Need to Workout with a Workout Mirror?

It ultimately depends on the model you purchase. Most only need a little more floor space than a typical workout mat. The only exceptions are higher-end models that use AI or special software to keep track of your workout movements. These often require a little more floor space—typically a few extra feet on all sides—than other less sophisticated models.

How Do You Clean a Workout Mirror?

Despite their high-tech designs, most workout mirrors are constructed using ordinary tempered glass. This not only makes them ultra-durable, but as easy to clean as any ordinary household mirror. Just grab your favorite non-streaking glass cleaner, spray a bit directly onto a clean washcloth, and wipe down your workout mirror like usual.
