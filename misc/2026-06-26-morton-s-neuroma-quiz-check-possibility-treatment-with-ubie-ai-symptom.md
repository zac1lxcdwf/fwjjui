What is Morton's Neuroma?

Morton's neuroma is a painful condition affecting the ball of your foot, between the third and fourth toes. It results from swollen and inflamed nerves leading to your foot's toes. It is often caused by shoes that are too tight or have high heels, which can compress and irritate the nerves in your foot.

Diagnostic Questions for Morton's Neuroma

Your doctor may ask these questions to check for this disease:

Do you experience numbness or tingling in your toes?

Are you experiencing toe pain?

Do you have tingling or numbness in your legs even when you're not moving?

Does pain improve with rest but worsen with movement?

Do you have difficulty walking normally?

Treatment of Morton's Neuroma

Custom-made insoles can help reduce pressure on the affected part of your foot. Your doctor might also prescribe anti-inflammatory oral medications and injections to alleviate pain. In severe cases, foot surgery may be recommended.
