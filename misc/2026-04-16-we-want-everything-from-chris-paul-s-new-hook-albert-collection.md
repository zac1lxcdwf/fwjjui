# We Want Everything From Chris Paul's New Hook & Albert Collection

When you think of Houston Rockets' Chris Paul, accessories may not be the first thing that come to mind. Get ready for that to change. Hook & Albert, self-proclaimed as The Accessories Company, has teamed up with Paul for a limited-edition collection that elevates its designs to baller status.

[Chris Paul's](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/1552394068.html)

[Paul's New Hook Albert](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/blog/3061714528.html)

The collaboration MVP is undoubtedly the Garment Weekender bag. An upscale garment bag disguised as a durable duffel, it can accommodate two of your suits, a pair of dress shoes plus the socks, shirts, and ties to match. Designed to make a sophisticate statement, you can buy this weekender in either inky black or rich gray pebble leather (a favorite around the Men's Health offices), with custom gold hardware.

Amp Up Your Work Style:

And if you needed a new backpack, Dopp kit, or lapel pins, those are in the collection, too. The Commuter Backpack expands when you need it or slims down when you don't. Perfect for a short commute or an add on for a long weekend, this bag keeps you organized with interior pockets for your laptop and tablet, large front pocket for grab-and-go items, and two hidden rear pockets for anything you need to keep especially safe like your wallet, keys, and cash. Add to that the extra thick padding on back and straps for ultimate comfort.

[Paul's New Hook](https://insulin-pathways.hbsnzj.com/)

As any well-traveled man knows, you can't go anywhere without your Dopp kit. Not only does the Leather Travel Dopp Kit open wide with a small internal pocket, it has two flat-folding side zip flaps with additional pockets to keep all the small things neat and orderly—maybe one of the lapel pins that add a finishing touch to the collaboration?

[From Chris](https://skinsequence.escortskhi.com/blog/6844260926.html)

[We Want Everything](https://thymosin-report.hbsnzj.com/)

[New Hook](https://repair-cascade.jcesqc8.com/blog/7514314939.html)

All of the products are available now on hookandalbert.com and at Saks Fifth Avenue, with prices ranging from $30 to $585. With any purchase from the collection, a portion of proceeds goes to the Chris Paul Family Foundation. Sounds like a slam dunk if you ask us.
