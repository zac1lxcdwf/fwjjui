# Welcome to the Stage, the Star Horse of 'Witcher'

Today, Hollywood welcomed into it's equine pantheon a new horse. One devoted fans following the tight-lipped rollout of Netflix's The Witcher have been eagerly anticipating. In a new promotional photo for the series which is expected to be released this fall, that The Witcher's star Henry Cavill also shared on his Instagram, fans got their first glimpse of Roach. Roach the horse is the faithful steed of Cavill's main character Geralt of Rivia that accompanies him on his solo adventures to slay monsters in both the video games and books and short stories by Andrzej Sapkowski upon which the new series is based.
