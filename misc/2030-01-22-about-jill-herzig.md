# About Jill Herzig

[About Jill Herzig](https://barrierwatch.bbsxyxy.com/blog/6032940421.html)

[Chief Brand Officer](https://immunopeptide.xianfengchem.com/)

Jill Herzig, Head of Brand Innovation

Jill is Co-founder and Head of Brand Innovation at Midi Health, where she oversees brand and collaborates with Midi’s clinical, user experience, product, and engineering teams to ensure Midi patients feel seen, heard, and supported at every touchpoint. Prior to Midi Jill held leadership positions at January AI, a startup in the diabetes space, and Weight Watchers, where she spearheaded content innovation. Before working on brands in the health space, Jill was the editor-in-chief of The Good Life and Redbook magazines, and executive editor of Glamour, where she harnessed the power of print, digital, and social media to motivate positive behavior change, build community, support social causes, and celebrate the extraordinary achievements of women and girls.
