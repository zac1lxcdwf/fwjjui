# Shanna Thomson, Midi Clinician

[Shanna Thomson Midi](https://benpepsi.com/)

[Thomson Midi Clinician](https://jointrevive.xianfengchem.com/blog/8539148834.html)

[Shanna Thomson Midi](https://musclemend.sunsimiaosd.com/)

Shanna Thomson, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

A path into women’s health: "I began my career in primary care at a Federally Qualified Health Center in 2012, where I spent five years caring for diverse communities. I later transitioned into reproductive healthcare and spent eight years supporting patients of all genders. Throughout my career, I’ve always been drawn to women’s health and the impact thoughtful care can have across every stage of life."

Part of a new movement: "What inspires me about working at Midi is the access, education, and options we can offer women in midlife. Too often, women have been expected to navigate this stage of life without clear answers or meaningful support. I’m proud to be part of a team that is changing that experience."

Care that’s collaborative: "My approach starts with understanding a patient’s concerns and identifying her goals. From there, we explore options together and create a plan that fits her symptoms, priorities, and life. I believe good care is rooted in trust, open communication, and shared decision-making. I want every patient to leave feeling heard, supported, and hopeful about the next steps in her care."
