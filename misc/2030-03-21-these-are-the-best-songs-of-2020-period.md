If 2020 were a Batman movie it would be Batman & Robin. What had so much potential (George Clooney! Arnold Schwarzenegger! Uma goddamn Thurman!) was promptly dashed by one hell of a plot-twist that quickly went south.

But like Batman & Robin, there's one silver lining amidst all the disappointment and frustration: a really, really good soundtrack. Although you won't see anything from The Smashing Pumpkins or Bone Thugs N Harmony on our list of the best songs of 2020 so far, you will find some throwbacks mixed in with modern classics.

2020 has brought back stalwarts like Fiona Apple, The Rolling Stones, and Bruce Springsteen. It's flooded Spotify with incredible dance tracks, like those by Disclosure. And it's produced a flight of female indie-rock bands and artists that tug and the heartstrings and pound at the temples all at the same time. (Here's looking at you Phoebe Bridgers.)

Our list of the best songs of 2020 may not be exactly what you expect from Men's Health, but if 2020 has a theme, it's the unexpected.
