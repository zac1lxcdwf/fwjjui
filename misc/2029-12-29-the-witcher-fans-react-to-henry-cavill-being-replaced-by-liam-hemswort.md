# ‘The Witcher’ Fans React to Henry Cavill Being Replaced by Liam Hemsworth

[Henry Cavill Being Replaced](https://taihpeptide.com/)

Henry Cavill will soon be back on our screens as Geralt of Rivia when The Witcher returns for its third season, but that will be his final appearance as the character. It has been confirmed by Netflix that the show has been renewed for a fourth season, but that the main character will be recast, with Hunger Games star Liam Hemsworth playing Geralt moving forward.

As with the greatest of literary characters, I pass the torch with reverence for the time spent embodying Geralt and enthusiasm to see Liam’s take on this most fascinating and nuanced of men. Liam, good sir, this character has such a wonderful depth to him, enjoy diving in and seeing what you can find.”
