# How Tom Cruise Helped Timothée Chalamet Train for 'Dune Part Two'

Ever since his breakout role in the romantic drama Call Me By Your Name, Timothée Chalamet has been hailed as one of the most exciting young up-and-coming actors in Hollywood, with roles spanning epic science fiction and family-friendly franchise fare (but famously "no superhero movies"). And when it comes to the performers whose careers he looks to for inspiration, he chooses those at the top of their game: like Tom Cruise.

In a new GQ profile, Chalamet revealed he has been hugely influenced by Cruise's recent cinematic output, especially the action sequel Top Gun: Maverick, which he watched multiple times while filming Dune, including holding a screening for the entire cast and crew.

"Top Gun was just hugely inspiring to me last summer when we were making Dune,” he said. “Some of the crew were kind of scoffing at going, but I just thought it was one of the greatest films I’ve ever seen."

When Chalamet then met the movie star in the flesh, it resulted in him receiving some career advice he has taken to heart.

"After I met Tom Cruise, right after finishing the first Dune, he sent me the most wonderfully inspiring email," Chalamet said. "He basically said, in Old Hollywood, you would be getting dance training and fight training, and nobody is going to hold you to that standard today. So it’s up to you. The email was really like a war cry."

This email reportedly included contact information for any kind of expert Chalamet might need for blockbuster stunt training, including coaches who would teach him how to drive a motorcycle and fly a helicopter (both of which Cruise has done with finesse in the recent Mission: Impossible movies).

It certainly looks as if Cruise's helpful note to Chalamet has paid off: auteur director Denis Villeneuve noted a marked difference in Chalamet's physical preparation and performance during the filming of Dune Part Two.

"Action-wise, I felt that he was much more trained than in Part One, and ready for the fighting sequences," he said. "I was impressed by his level of discipline for Part Two. You know, when you are the lead on a movie, there’s a presence, the way you approach your work and your discipline will necessarily have a ripple effect on the rest of the crew. He was the first one on set, always ready. And I was super pleased and impressed with how Timothée really embraced that discipline and became, for me, a real leading actor on this film."
