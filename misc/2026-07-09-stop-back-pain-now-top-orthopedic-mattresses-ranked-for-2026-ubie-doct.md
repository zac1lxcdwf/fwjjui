Support & Spinal AlignmentA good orthopedic mattress evenly distributes your weight, keeping your spine in a neutral "S-curve" from neck to hips.

Pressure ReliefMaterials like memory foam or zoned coils cushion shoulders, hips, and lower back without letting you sink too deeply.

DurabilityHigh-density foams, quality coils, or natural latex resist sagging, so you maintain proper support for years.

Firmness LevelMost experts recommend a medium-firm mattress for back pain—firm enough to support natural alignment, soft enough to cradle pressure points.

Tips for Maximizing Spinal Support

Even the best mattress can't work miracles if your sleep setup isn't optimized. Keep these pointers in mind:

Sleep PositionBack sleepers: Place a small pillow under your knees.Side sleepers: Use a thicker pillow between knees to keep hips aligned.Stomach sleepers: Try transitioning to back or side. If you must, use a very thin pillow under your pelvis.

Pillow ChoiceMatch pillow loft and firmness to your sleep position: medium-loft for back sleepers, high-loft for side sleepers.

Mattress RotationRotate your mattress head-to-toe every 3–6 months. This prevents uneven wear and sagging.

Adequate FoundationUse a sturdy bed frame or foundation that prevents sagging. A slatted base should have slats no more than 3 inches apart.

Keep ActiveStrengthening your core and back muscles through gentle exercise (yoga, pilates, walking) helps maintain proper posture on and off the mattress.

When to Seek Professional Advice

Numbness, tingling, or weakness in your legs
