What are some effective ways to relieve restless leg syndrome symptoms?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Restless leg syndrome (RLS) can often be managed at home with a few simple strategies:

Regular exercise: Moderate daily activity like walking or stretching can reduce symptoms.

Leg massages: Massaging the calves and thighs helps ease discomfort.

Warm baths: Soaking in warm water relaxes muscles and may improve sleep.

Medication: If lifestyle changes aren't enough, a doctor may prescribe medication to manage persistent symptoms.

Because RLS can overlap with other conditions—such as iron deficiency, nerve issues, or sleep disorders—it's important to understand what's actually driving your symptoms before choosing a treatment path.
