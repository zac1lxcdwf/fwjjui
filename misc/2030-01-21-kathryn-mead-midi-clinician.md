# Kathryn Mead, Midi Clinician

[Midi Clinician](https://mimi567.com/uo8q9nm5b1/)

[Kathryn Mead Midi](https://aegispeptide.snaketongueofficial.com/)

[Mead Midi Clinician](https://lifegoesom.com/223fas4/)

Kathryn Mead, MSN, APRN, WHNP-BC

Grounded in women’s health: "I began my healthcare career in 2013, and over the years I’ve gained experience in community health, gynecology, labor and delivery, and functional medicine, work that gave me a front-row view into the many stages of women’s lives. To me, women’s health isn’t just a specialty, it’s the foundation of every healthy community."

Every journey is different: "What drew me to Midi was the chance to support women through difficult transitions and help them live more comfortably. I believe the most powerful care starts by truly listening. Every woman has her own story, and my role is to honor that journey while helping her navigate symptoms with clarity and confidence. Listening with intention helps me uncover what’s really going on and tailor care that truly fits each woman’s life."

Holistic, empathetic care: "My approach is empathetic and holistic, drawing on many aspects of health to create a care plan that feels realistic and sustainable. At the end of a visit, I want women to feel heard, supported, and empowered to take the next step in their health. When care is collaborative and practical, it leads to meaningful, lasting change."
