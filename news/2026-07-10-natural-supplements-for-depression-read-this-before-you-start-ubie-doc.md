# Natural Supplements for Depression: Read This Before You Start | Ubie Doctor's Note

Published on: 7/21/2026

Are natural supplements safe for depression?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Natural supplements may help ease symptoms of depression, but their safety and effectiveness vary widely from person to person. Some options, like St. John's Wort, omega-3 fatty acids, SAM-e, and vitamin D, have shown potential benefits in research, while others lack strong evidence or may interact with medications. Because supplements are not regulated as strictly as prescription drugs, quality and dosing can be inconsistent. Always consult a healthcare provider before starting any supplement, especially if you take other medications or have underlying health conditions.

Explanation

Natural supplements are sometimes used for depression, but their safety and effectiveness can be different for each person. Some studies suggest that certain herbal medicines might help with depression and anxiety. However, not all supplements have been tested enough to know if they are truly safe or work well.

Important Points:

Effectiveness: Some herbal medicines show promise in helping with depression, but more research is needed to confirm their benefits.

Safety: The safety of natural supplements can vary. Some might interact with other medicines or have side effects.

Consult a Doctor: It's important to talk to a doctor before starting any supplement, especially if you are already taking other medications.
