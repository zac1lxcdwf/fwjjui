Narrow stools, often described as pencil-thin or ribbon-like, can signal a range of underlying conditions. Common causes include obstructions in the colon or rectum, such as tumors, polyps, strictures, or scarring from inflammation. Less serious causes may involve constipation, hemorrhoids, or irritable bowel syndrome (IBS).

Because narrow stools can occasionally point to serious conditions like colorectal cancer, it's important not to ignore persistent changes in stool shape.
