Explanation

The sensation of your upper stomach feeling empty right after eating could be due to a condition called visceral hypersensitivity. This means your stomach and intestines are more sensitive to feelings of fullness or emptiness.

One study suggests that consuming cold water might trigger this hypersensitivity in some people. When you drink cold water, it can cause your stomach to feel different, even when there is food inside.

If this feeling continues or bothers you, it might be helpful to pay attention to what you eat and drink and see if certain things make it worse. If you have more questions or concerns, talking to a healthcare provider can be a good next step.
