As the show was filmed over the course of three live performances during the musical’s first year on Broadway, fans can expect to see the original cast (who made headlines for its diverse, non-white representation of the Founding Fathers) grace their screens. Well, given that the show does have a few swear words and it will be streaming on Disney+ (with a PG-13 rating), Miranda revealed on Twitter that yes, there will be some minor changes to the show. But don’t worry—it won’t be anything that will significantly alter the story.
