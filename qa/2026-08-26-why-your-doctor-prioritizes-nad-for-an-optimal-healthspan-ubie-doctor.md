What is NAD+ and why does it matter for aging? NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a vital cellular coenzyme that powers mitochondrial energy production, activates DNA repair enzymes, and regulates longevity pathways. NAD+ levels decline by up to 50% with age, contributing to fatigue, metabolic slowdown, and increased disease risk.

How can you boost NAD+ naturally? Doctors recommend evidence-based strategies including regular exercise, calorie management, intermittent fasting, and NAD+ precursor supplements like nicotinamide riboside (NR) or nicotinamide mononucleotide (NMN) to safely support healthspan and cellular function.

While early research is promising, outcomes depend on individual factors like health status, medications, and supplement quality. What Is NAD+ and Why It MattersNAD+ acts like a molecular battery in your cells:

It accepts and donates electrons during metabolism, powering mitochondria (the cell's "engine").

It activates sirtuins, proteins that regulate aging, inflammation, and stress resistance.

It supports PARPs, enzymes critical for repairing DNA damage.

As NAD+ levels decline with age, these vital processes slow down, contributing to fatigue, metabolic decline, and increased risk of chronic disease.

NAD+ Decline: A Hallmark of AgingMultiple studies show NAD+ levels drop by up to 50% between age 20 and 80. Consequences include:

Reduced mitochondrial function, leading to lower energy and endurance.

Impaired DNA repair, increasing the risk of cancer and age-related disorders.

Weakened stress response, making cells more vulnerable to inflammation and oxidative damage.

Boosting NAD+ helps counteract these effects, improving cellular resilience and overall vitality.

Key Benefits of NAD+ for Longevity and HealthspanResearch—both in animals and early human trials—suggests that raising NAD+ can:

Increased ATP production means more energy for daily activities and exercise.

Better genome maintenance may slow aging-related mutations.

SIRT1 and SIRT3 promote metabolic health, lower inflammation, and improve heart function.

Enhanced insulin sensitivity, weight management, and lipid profiles.

Potentially supports cognitive function and may slow neurodegenerative processes.
