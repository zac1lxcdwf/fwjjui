Explanation

[stomach Ubie](https://3899241.com/ieojyjcs/)

Feeling sick, tired, and having a bloated stomach can be caused by different health issues. Gastroparesis is a condition where the stomach takes too long to empty its contents. This can lead to feeling tired, as noted in the medical literature. People with gastroparesis often feel bloated and sick because their stomachs are not moving food along as they should.

Another possible cause is gallstone disease. Gallstones are hard pieces that form in the gallbladder, which can cause stomach pain and bloating. Sometimes, people with gallstones feel sick and tired because their bodies are trying to deal with the discomfort and pain.

Both of these conditions can make someone feel unwell and tired. If you or someone you know is experiencing these symptoms, it might be helpful to talk to a healthcare professional to find out more about what could be causing them and how to feel better.
