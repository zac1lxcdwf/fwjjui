Internal Medicine

Missed periods without an obvious cause are often linked to hormonal shifts triggered by stress, weight changes, or intense physical activity. Other possible causes include thyroid issues, polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), or pregnancy. Because irregular or absent periods can signal an underlying health condition, it's important to consult a doctor for proper evaluation.

Explanation

When your period stops unexpectedly, one thing to consider is that your body might be sending signals that it isn't ready for a cycle.

[for No Reason](https://ppmaas.com/r0vfr04/)

This can happen if you are under a lot of stress, have lost or gained too much weight, or if you are exercising a lot. These factors can change the hormone signals from your brain that tell your body when to have your period. This condition is sometimes called functional hypothalamic amenorrhea. It means that while your body is healthy, it isn't making enough of the hormones needed to start the menstrual cycle.

Other reasons might include issues with your thyroid or other hormone imbalances. It is also a good idea to think about any changes you have made recently, like a new workout routine or changes in your eating habits, which might have affected your cycle.
