A house call doctor visit is when your doctor comes to you rather than requiring you go visit them at their clinic or office location. House call visits can be especially useful for patients who may need routine checkups (e.g., physicals, blood tests) that require less time or if they have limited mobility and would otherwise have trouble getting around.

Benefits

Why should you consider getting a house call doctor visit? It’s simple: convenience and privacy. When it comes to convenience, there’s no need for you or your loved one to leave home for an appointment with a physician. Instead, your doctor will come directly to your home, office, or hotel room. This saves time and money by eliminating travel expenses and time lost from work or school commitments. In addition, when you receive care at home, you can remain comfortable in familiar surroundings without going through the hassle of driving somewhere unfamiliar. Privacy is another major benefit of receiving medical care at home. A house call doctor visit ensures that all medical information remains private because it is never shared with anyone else.
