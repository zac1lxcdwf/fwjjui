Published on: 7/21/2026

What are the common reasons for getting fat despite a healthy lifestyle?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Unexplained weight gain despite a healthy lifestyle is common and often caused by factors beyond diet and exercise. Genetics influence how your body stores fat, hormonal changes (such as thyroid imbalances, insulin resistance, or shifts during menopause) can slow metabolism, and individual differences in energy use mean two people eating the same foods may gain weight differently. Medications, stress, and sleep quality also play a role.

If you're gaining weight without a clear reason, it's worth investigating the underlying cause rather than guessing.
