Can poor vision cause headaches?

Reviewed by Yoshinori Abe, MD

Internal Medicine

Yes, poor vision can cause headaches. When your eyes strain to focus—due to uncorrected refractive errors, presbyopia, or conditions like astigmatism—the surrounding muscles overwork, triggering tension-type headaches, brow pain, or discomfort behind the eyes. Symptoms often worsen after reading, screen use, or driving.
