Intermittent Chest Pain Relieved by Gas: When to Seek Urgent Care?

10s, Male

I have been having chest pain that seems to be on and off throughout the day for about a week. It is a sharp pain that feels like it clogs my mid-chest. When I burp or pass gas, it seems to provide some relief, but it doesn't make the pain go away completely. What are the danger signs that would require me to seek urgent care or go to the ER?

Dr. Taylor’s Response

I understand you're experiencing intermittent chest pain that's been ongoing for about a week, with some relief when you release gas. This symptom can be concerning, and I appreciate you asking about it.

In my experience, chest pain should always be taken seriously, especially if it's new or persistent. While your symptoms might be related to digestive issues, it's crucial not to assume this without proper medical evaluation.

Danger signs that warrant immediate medical attention (call 911 or go to the ER) include:

Severe, crushing, or squeezing chest pain

Pain spreading to your jaw, left arm, or back
