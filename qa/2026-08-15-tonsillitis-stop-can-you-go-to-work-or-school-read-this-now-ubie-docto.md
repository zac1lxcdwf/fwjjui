Tonsillitis is often contagious, so staying home from work or school is generally recommended to prevent spreading the infection and to allow your body the rest it needs to recover. Most doctors advise remaining home until you've been fever-free for at least 24 hours and, if prescribed antibiotics, taking them for a full 24 hours before returning to normal activities.

Explanation

Tonsillitis is an illness that can make your throat hurt and give you a fever. It is important to stay home if you have tonsillitis so you don't spread germs to other people at work or school.

Here are some reasons why staying home is a good idea:

Resting Helps You Heal: When you are sick, your body needs time to fight off the germs. Resting at home helps you get better faster.

Preventing the Spread of Germs: Tonsillitis can be caused by germs that spread easily to others. By staying home, you help keep your friends and classmates safe from getting sick, too.

Listening to Your Body: If you feel too tired or have a fever, it's a sign that your body needs a break. Not sure if your symptoms require staying home? Take a quick symptom checker test to better understand what your body might be telling you.

Taking care of yourself and staying home when you're sick is important for your health and the health of those around you.
