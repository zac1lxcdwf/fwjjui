1. Get hydrated quickly

Getting hydrated should be the first thing you do when you’ve had one drink too many. A glass of water can go a long way towards raising your spirits and helping you recover from the symptoms of a hangover. Alcohol is a diuretic, meaning it increases the volume of urine made by the kidney and passed through the body. Precious fluids are also lost through sweat or if you experience diarrhea or vomiting as a symptom after a night of heavy drinking.

Remaining hydrating – while – drinking can also aid in minimizing those aftereffects. Alternating a full glass of water in between cocktails is a helpful trick to help avoid hangover symptoms. It’s good practice to remember that having a hydrating beverage once you get back home can provide a tremendous boost for your body before calling it a night.

We recommend starting with a full glass of water before moving onto an electrolyte rich beverage, coconut water, or kombucha. Regardless of what you choose, staying hydrated is a good practice towards finding some relief from your hangover troubles.

2. Attempt to eat some food

One significant factor in many hangovers is a steep decline in blood sugar levels. Lactic acid in the body spikes as our bodies break down and absorb alcohol, which impacts our blood sugar levels. Having a bite to eat while hungover can work towards reestablishing normal blood sugar levels, soothe nausea and can help fight off those lethargic feelings. Having some food before you drink – or nibbling on something throughout the night – can help prevent hangover symptoms.

Something high in carbohydrates and healthy fats like toast with butter or jam, spicy Thai food, or a steaming bowl of Pho can propel alcohol from your system quickly and get you on the road to recovery.

3. Take some antioxidants

Drinking alcohol causes our bodies to generate free radicals through a process referred to as oxidative stress. Oxidative stress has been linked to health concerns such as cancers and heart disease. Antioxidants are helpful compounds capable of getting rid of excess free radicals.

Consuming foods that are high in antioxidants can vastly diminish the unfavorable effects of overindulging in alcohol. Some qualified candidates looking to boost your antioxidant levels while in the midst of a hangover include ginger, pomegranate, berries, dark chocolate, nuts, and carrots.

4. Grab some medications or supplements

There are many over the counter drugs, supplements, and medications for those suffering from a hangover, some of which claim to help improve symptoms quickly but don’t actually make a difference. Fortunately, there are some medicines that can help ward off the nasty symptoms of drinking too much.

Aspirin and anti-inflammatories like ibuprofen can help ease the pain of muscle aches and headaches by reducing the inflammation caused by alcohol.

Antacids work by minimizing the indigestion, heartburn, and nausea brought on by over-drinking, making them a helpful option for those suffering from a hangover.

Red ginseng is a staple of Chinese medicine that has been used for centuries to treat conditions such as asthma and anxiety. This cherished root can also help relieve hangover symptoms such as nausea, feelings of lightheadedness, and headaches.
