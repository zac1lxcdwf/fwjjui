First, the sunscreen basics. This serum has an SPF level of 50, based primarily on zinc oxide and homosalate, along with sprinkles of octisalate and octocrylene. The TLDR version is that this is a hybrid sunscreen, with mineral protection from zinc oxide and chemical protection from homosalate, octisalate, and octocrylene.

Of course, sun protection is only part of the equation when it comes to a sunscreen’s sales pitch. For me, simply blocking UV rays isn’t enough to justify the investment. It’s not that this isn’t important, because it is (unless you want early signs of aging and an increased risk of skin cancer).

There are just so many better options out there now that include ingredients for additional benefits—and this is where TimeSavvy takes it to the next level. As someone whose biggest skin concern is redness—especially after being in the sun—Hybrid Sheer is loaded with two of my favorite ingredients for combating my innate Irish ruddiness.

There’s niacinamide, which is one of the best ingredients to improve the health of your skin barrier and minimize visible redness. Then there’s aloe vera, a classic natural anti-inflammatory hydration booster. With centella asiatica for added hydration and collagen to improve elasticity, this SPF serum doesn’t just fight against the negative effects of UV rays; it offers unrelated everyday benefits.

Feel and Effectiveness

The concept of an SPF serum (versus a standard sunscreen or SPF moisturizer) is that it’s thinner and absorbs faster; however, most tend to overlook this notion and present as a standard sunscreen with a cuter name.

TimeSavvy is one of the few that actually live up to the original SPF serum billing. Given that it’s such a stark white, mimicking your drugstore SPF, I was expecting a heavy, clunky cream.

Instead, it’s watery in the best way and absorbs in seconds. Honestly, if you applied it with my eyes closed, I would almost think it’s closer to a gel consistently, similar to the classic and famously invisible Supergoop! Unseen. Plus, there is no white cast or lingering greasiness.

I also have sensitive skin and it never caused a reaction. My only minor complaint is that it is neither water- nor sweat-resistant. So it’s not a good option for swimming or going for a run, but as an everyday SPF, it’s as comfortable as they come.
