Explanation

Left lower abdominal pain can have several causes. One common cause is diverticulitis, which is inflammation of small pouches in the colon. This condition often presents with pain, fever, and changes in bowel habits.

Other possible causes include gynecological issues such as ovarian cysts or endometriosis, which can cause pain in the lower abdomen. Additionally, gastrointestinal problems like irritable bowel syndrome (IBS) or constipation can also lead to discomfort in this area.
