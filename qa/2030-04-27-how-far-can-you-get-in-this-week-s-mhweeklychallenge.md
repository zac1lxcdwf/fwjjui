# ​​How Far Can You Get In This Week’s #MHWeeklyChallenge?

Each week, Men’s Health posts a new fitness challenge on Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter. Compete with yourself, your friends, and others on social media, and let us know how you do by sharing your results. Be sure to include the #MHWeeklyChallenge hashtag in your posts; you can also search that hashtag at any time to find previous challenges.

[In This](https://copperglow.dns0663.com/blog/3578904868.html)

A single pushup may not be a challenge, but how about 250 of them?

Relax, we’re not tasking you with a single set of 250 pushups. (Is that even possible?)

This week’s MH Weekly Challenge comes from Equinox personal training manager David Otey, a recent Men’s Health Trainer of the Day. Recently, Otey stopped by the MH Rec Room (#MHRecRoom) to drop off this monster of a challenge.

[Get In This Week](https://allpepay.com/blog/4777956006.html)

The entire thing revolves around one fundamental exercise—the pushup—but don’t let that lead you to believe it’s for everyone. In fact, if you can complete it, you are without a doubt one of the elite. “If you’re able to do the 25 reps for 10 sets, you’re a beast,” Otey says.

Watch the video above for Otey’s play-by-play breakdown of this week's challenge. Simply put, you’re going for 10 sets (or as many sets as possible) of 25 pushups with one minute of rest between sets. Like we said, nail this one and you’re a certified monster.

Of course, this challenge is not about just getting through all 250 pushups by any means necessary. Pay attention to your form; the last thing you want to do is repeat an ugly, potentially harmful pushup over and over, all the way into three-digit territory. (Check out this article to make sure you aren’t making these common pushup mistakes.)

A few quick pointers: Keep your wrists below your shoulders, your neck in line with your spine, and your elbows tucked close to your sides. And remember to inhale on your way down and exhale on your way back up. That last one will help you out more than you think.

Try your hand at this week’s challenge and let us (@menshealthmag) and Otey (@davidscottpt) know how you did! Be sure to tag your photos and videos on social media with the hashtag #MHWeeklyChallenge.
