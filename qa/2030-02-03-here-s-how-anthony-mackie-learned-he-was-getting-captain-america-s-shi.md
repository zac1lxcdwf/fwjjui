The release of Avengers: Endgame this past April was also an endgame of sorts for some of the Marvel actors we've grown to love over the years. Chris Evans and Robert Downey Jr. hung up their mantels as Captain America and Iron Man, respectively—and one of the last scenes in Endgame shows Captain America giving his legendary shield to Falcon, played by Men's Health July/August cover guy Anthony Mackie.

In the comics, Falcon replaced Steve Rogers as Captain America back in 2014, but the MCU crew didn't officially decide to hand him the shield until three years ago. "We don't want anything to leak, so we keep everything a secret from the actors, especially Mackie," co-director Joe Russo told Men's Health.

[How Anthony Mackie](https://glycometrix.snaketongueofficial.com/blog/4470133433.html)

Mackie didn't find out that Falcon was getting Captain America's shield until the filming of Endgame—and his buddy Chris Evans had a big part in how he found out. Mackie was over at Evans' house watching football with some friends, but he hadn't had a chance to see the full Endgame script yet. Evans was the one to show it to him. "Chris gets the look of a six-year-old kid on his face, runs away, and comes back with the script. I’m reading it, and it dawns on me what’s happening," Mackie told Men's Health.

And then he was on set, preparing to wield the coveted shield—and that's when it finally felt real. "They were like, 'Are you ready for tomorrow? You're getting your shield,'" he said. "I'm like, 'Get the fuck out of here.'"

He added that the experience "was like winning the Oscar. I was nervous. I was excited. I was stumbling over words."
