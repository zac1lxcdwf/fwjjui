Explanation

When you drink alcohol, it can cause your stomach to feel bloated for a few reasons. Alcohol can irritate your stomach lining and intestines, which can make them swell up and feel uncomfortable. This irritation can disrupt the normal function of your digestive system.

How Alcohol Affects the Stomach

Irritation: Alcohol can irritate the lining of the stomach, leading to inflammation.

Delayed Emptying: It can slow down how quickly your stomach empties, causing a feeling of fullness.

Gas Production: Alcohol can increase the production of gas in the intestines.

Acid Production: It can also affect the amount of acid in your stomach, which might lead to discomfort.
