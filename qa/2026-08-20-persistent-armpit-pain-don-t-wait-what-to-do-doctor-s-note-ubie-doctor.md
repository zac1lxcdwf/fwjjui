Explanation

When pain in your armpit lasts a long time (more than 2 weeks), you should talk with your doctor to find out why it is happening. Persistent pain might be caused by conditions like nerve irritation, a mild injury, or sometimes issues related to lymph nodes or even after previous surgery.

Your doctor may ask about your symptoms, when the pain started, and if you've noticed any changes in the area. They might also perform a physical exam and recommend tests or imaging studies like an ultrasound or CT scan to check for signs of infection, inflammation, or other issues.

In some cases, conditions like axillary web syndrome could be a reason for ongoing discomfort and may need therapy or other treatments. It is important not to ignore the pain because early diagnosis can help stop a minor problem from becoming more serious.
