Explanation

Foot pain can significantly affect your walking pattern. Common conditions such as plantar fasciitis can cause pain in the heel or arch. When it hurts to stand or move, you may start limping or avoiding weight on the affected foot. This altered movement pattern can gradually weaken your muscles, leading to reduced strength and balance. There are several effective solutions for managing foot pain. Well-cushioned shoes and custom orthotics (special shoe inserts) provide support and reduce discomfort. Regular stretching and strengthening exercises enhance foot and leg muscle flexibility and strength. It's important to limit activities that worsen the pain to prevent the underlying condition from deteriorating. Over-the-counter pain relievers can provide additional relief, and in some cases, a physical therapist or healthcare provider may recommend specialized treatments. These approaches, when combined, help improve support, reduce pain, and make walking more comfortable.
