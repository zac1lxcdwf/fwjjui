What is the term for the partial or complete loss of hair that occurs most commonly on the scalp?

Reviewed by Suo Lee, MD

Internal Medicine

The term for partial or complete loss of hair, especially on the scalp, is 'alopecia.'

Important Next Steps

Explanation

Alopecia is the medical term used to describe hair loss, which can occur partially or completely, and is most commonly seen on the scalp. There are different types of alopecia, each with unique characteristics and causes.

Types of Alopecia:

Androgenetic Alopecia: This is the most common form, often referred to as male or female pattern baldness.

Alopecia Areata: An autoimmune condition that causes patchy hair loss.
