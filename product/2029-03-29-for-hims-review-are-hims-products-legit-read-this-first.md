Erectile dysfunction

Hims treats men for several common sexual health conditions. Men sometimes find these subjects challenging to discuss in person in a doctor’s office, which is one reason so many men prefer the privacy of a telemedicine approach for issues like erectile dysfunction (ED)

Hims addresses ED with an array of medications, many available for a fraction of the price you would pay via traditional routes. Pills designed for daily use arrive regularly on a subscription plan. Those intended for use soon before intercourse are billed per anticipated monthly use and come in quantities determined by you and your doctor.

All of Hims ED medications are PDE-5 inhibitors.1 These drugs prevent an enzyme from signaling to your body that it’s time for an erection to stop. In men with ED, these signals can be mistimed and cause an erection to subside before climax or prevent an erection from occurring in the first place.

Hims offers a wider array of these treatment options than its close competitors. Here’s a quick look at the ED medications you can get from Hims:

Sildenafil

Premature ejaculation

An erection is a fine thing to have, but if you suffer from premature ejaculation, it can feel like all that engorgement is going to waste. And you don’t have to have clinically diagnosed premature ejaculation to experience the shame and performance anxiety finishing early can bring.5

Fortunately, Hims addresses PE with several effective treatments used either separately or in combination.

Delay wipes

Hims partnered with Promescent, the originators of one of the safest, most reliable delay products on the market, to supply them with a delay spray and delay wipes. Products such as these apply a small amount of topical anesthetic to the penis, numbing it enough to increase ejaculation latency time (ELT) but not enough to take away the pleasurable feelings involved in sex.

Delay sprays can transfer to others for up to 15 minutes, so if you use a spray, make sure to let it set before intercourse — especially if you’re in a relationship and not using condoms. It doesn’t matter how long you last if your partner can’t feel you.

In 2024, Hims decided to discontinue the Climax Delay Spray product, focusing solely on its PE wipes. Though we are sad to see the delay spray option discontinued, people seeking specifically a spray can still get the same product from Promescent (but with what we've found to be a better spray mechanism).

Prescription SSRIs

Selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) are a class of antidepressants that increase the amount of serotonin circulating in your system. They’ve long been studied as a potential solution for premature ejaculation, and they’ve had a lot of success in that department.9 But they also offer more potential side effects than the topical anesthetics you’ll find in the wipes and sprays.

Here’s a look at the two SSRIs Hims offers for premature ejaculation:

Sertraline

Sertraline (generic Zoloft) delayed orgasm for a majority of men in a review of clinical trials, with sexual satisfaction rates above 50% compared to below 20% in control groups.10 Gastrointestinal side effects were common.

Paroxetine

Paroxetine (the active ingredient in Paxil) works by the same mechanism as sertraline but has fewer common side effects.11 Its price is also identical. Ultimately, the subtle differences between them will determine which is most effective and safe for you to use.

A 3-month supply of either medication ships quarterly for $72. This is identical to the price you’d pay for Roman’s sertraline, and you can’t get paroxetine from Roman.

Several studies have pointed out that certain ED medications can also delay orgasm in men, so Hims offers sildenafil (the generic form of Viagra) among its PE treatments.12 But doctors won’t typically recommend the use of ED medications for PE in men without concurrent erectile dysfunction, at least not before other treatment paths have been exhausted. There are too many potential side effects associated with ED meds, including the development of psychogenic ED in men who previously had no history of dysfunction.29

Hims FAQ

Is Hims just for hair loss?

Hims is definitely not just for hair loss. The company offers treatments for hair loss, erectile dysfunction, premature ejaculation, mental health concerns, and skincare.

Is Hims just for ED?

Hims offers a lot more than just ED treatments. You can get treatments for hair loss, premature ejaculation, and erectile dysfunction, as well as services like mental health care, and skincare.

Are Hims treatments safe?

The majority of Hims products — both prescription and non-prescription — have good track records in clinical trials as being effective and relatively safe. Certain medications have well-established side effect profiles and contraindications, and the fact that you have to consult with a doctor to get a prescription through its platform helps ensure you won’t end up taking something that could harm you.

Can Hims cure ED or hair loss?

Unfortunately, there are no cures for either hair loss or ED, but the treatments available can make their symptoms all but nonexistent. Common ED drugs can reliably help you achieve an erection sufficient for penetrative sex, and popular hair loss treatments can stop hair loss and even regrow some hair in most men. But if you stop taking any of these medications, they stop working.

Does Hims take insurance?

Hims doesn’t take insurance for any of its products or services at this time, but certain medications — specifically those prescribed through its primary care interface — may be eligible for insurance coverage when you pick them up at a local pharmacy and present your information.
