# Arnold Schwarzenegger Shared His Simple Rule for Dealing With Stress

With a career spanning movies, politics, pro bodybuilding and sports over several decades, Arnold Schwarzenegger is the definition of booked and busy, and it's probably fair to say that having such a packed schedule can be pretty overwhelming at times. In his newsletter, while responding to a fan's question about how he handles stress, Schwarzenegger explained his simple tactic for handling a hectic workload.

"I focus on one thing at a time," he wrote. "For instance, while I am writing this newsletter, my friends showed up to start reading my TV scripts and go on a bike ride. I told them to please go in the other room and play chess. I don’t want to be thinking about going to the gym on our bikes or rehearsing my scripts. I am thinking about my newsletter right now, and that’s it and that’s all. Then I will go on a bike ride to the gym and I won’t think about all of you. Then I will rehearse, and I won’t think about the gym."

"Don’t try to solve every problem at once," he continued. "Split things up into what you can conquer, and then move into the next thing."

In previous newsletters, Schwarzenegger has elaborated how he stays motivated and energized in both his working life and his physical training. "You are not a failure unless you completely give up," he said on the subject of building new habits and sticking to resolutions. "So as you hit the wall and think about giving up, think back to the reason you wanted to change."
